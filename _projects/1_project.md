I assembled a SO-101 robot arm and have already set up a basic imitation learning pipeline. I collected some demonstration data at my home in Japan and plan to bring the setup with me to the dorm.

To support future data collection and robot learning experiments, I also prepared the necessary infrastructure, including head-mounted and wrist-mounted cameras, shelving, and anti-slip mats for consistent manipulation tasks.

The goal is to have a personal setup for collecting data and experimenting with robot learning and VLA models outside the lab.
