A paper I co-authored, [“CLAP: Direct VLM-to-VLA Adaptation via Language-Action Grounding”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08974), has been accepted to the 10th Annual Conference on Robot Learning (CoRL 2026).

I worked on this project during my research internship at [OMRON SINIC X](https://www.omron.com/sinicx/en/). CLAP investigates the direct adaptation of pretrained vision-language models into vision-language-action models for robot control without architectural modifications or an additional action expert. The method prepends a natural-language description of an action sequence to the corresponding numeric action tokens, helping preserve the capabilities of the pretrained model.

Project page: <https://omron-sinicx.github.io/clap/>
